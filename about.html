ABOUT

This website is an author-driven project born from the desire to present digital comics in a way that goes beyond the limits of the printed page. Here you'll find a collection of original comics spanning different genres and tones, from comedy to adventure and drama, each crafted as a self-contained story with its own distinct visual identity. The goal of this site is to offer readers a simple, clear, and enjoyable way to read comics directly in the browser, regardless of the device they're using. Alongside the comics themselves, the site also offers a glimpse into their creation process, from initial idea to finished illustration.
