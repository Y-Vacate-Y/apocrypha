Description

Hired adventurers whose reputation far outstrips their actual abilities, and whose ineptitude at resolving even the most basic conflicts leads to comic situations. The characters don't fit the usual roles typical of the fantasy genre — expect the "great heroes" to bumble their way through problems a competent rookie could solve in minutes.
